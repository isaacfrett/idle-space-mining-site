Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Idle Space Mining has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Your game is saved on your own device and never uploaded.

The one exception is the optional Supply Drop reward video. If you choose to watch one, Google serves that ad and receives some information in order to do so. That is the only third-party data collection in the app, and it only happens when you tap the button.

What the app collects

Nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no user profiles. It never asks for your name, email, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos, because it does not use them.

Advertising

The app contains exactly one advertisement: the Supply Drop, a rewarded video you choose to watch from the Shop in exchange for a temporary production boost. There are no banners, no interstitials, and nothing that interrupts play. If you never tap it, no ad is ever shown and no ad request is ever made on your behalf.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To deliver, cap, measure and protect an ad against fraud, Google receives technical information such as your IP address, device type and operating system version, and whether the ad was watched. This is described in Google's Privacy & Terms and how Google uses data from partner apps.

Ads are non-personalised. The app explicitly requests non-personalised advertising on every request, so ads are not selected based on a profile of you or your interests.

The app does not track you. It does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and does not read your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). Your activity is not linked to you or followed across other apps and websites. Apple's SKAdNetwork may be used to measure ad performance in aggregate; it is designed by Apple so that installs can be counted without identifying individual people.

Ads are limited to content rated G.

Where your game data lives

Your progress is saved in a file on your own device. It is never uploaded anywhere. It is included in your device backup if you back up your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. Apple tells the app whether a purchase succeeded — nothing more. Payment details never reach this app or its developer. Apple's handling of that data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Network access

The app makes no network requests to any server operated by its developer, because there is no such server. Network traffic is limited to Apple's StoreKit framework talking to the App Store for purchases, and Google's advertising SDK when you choose to watch a Supply Drop.

Children

Idle Space Mining is not directed at children. It contains advertising and in-app purchases, and ads are restricted to content rated G. Because the app does not track users or read advertising identifiers, it does not build a profile of any player, including a child.

Your choices

Watching a Supply Drop is entirely optional and the game can be played and completed without ever watching one. You can also limit ad measurement system-wide in Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising and Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Changes

If this ever changes — for example if analytics, accounts or leaderboards are added — this policy will be updated before the feature ships, and the App Store privacy labels will be updated to match.
